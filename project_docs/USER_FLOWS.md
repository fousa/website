# USER_FLOWS.md

## Flow 1: The Professional Introduction (Primary)
The goal is to guide the visitor in one smooth motion from 'Who is this?' to 'I am impressed'.

1.  **Landing**:
    *   User opens `fousa.be`.
    *   Sees a clean Hero section with Name, Short Tagline ("Senior Full Stack Developer"), and a clear photo/avatar.
    *   *Interaction*: Scrolls down.

2.  **The Journey (Timeline)**:
    *   As scrolling happens, the vertical timeline appears.
    *   Items (Work Experiences) fade in from the side or detach from the background (Parallax).
    *   User sees company logos, job titles, and periods.
    *   *Action*: User stops at an interesting item and reads the short summary.

3.  **Portfolio Deep-dive**:
    *   Interspersed in the timeline (or alongside it), blocks with 'Key Projects' appear.
    *   User sees the distinction between "Freelance assignment at Company X" and "Personal Work".
    *   *Interaction*: Clicks on a project for more details (or sees details in a modal/expand).

4.  **Conversion**:
    *   At the bottom (or via a floating button), the user sees "Let's work together".
    *   *Action*: Clicks on 'Download CV' or goes to LinkedIn.

## Flow 2: Mobile Experience
The goal is information provision without frustration over complex animations.

1.  **Landing**:
    *   Simple, large typography. Clear "Menu" button or direct links.
2.  **Scrolling**:
    *   No heavy 3D parallax effects.
    *   Timeline is a clean vertical list (line on left, content on right).
    *   Projects are stacked in cards.
3.  **Contact**:
    *   Large tappable targets for Email and Socials at the bottom of the screen (sticky footer possible).
