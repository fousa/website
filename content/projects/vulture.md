A flight planning and analysis app for glider pilots across all Apple devices. 

Plan tasks with SkySight weather integration, record flights on iPhone and Apple Watch, and analyze your performance with detailed statistics.

Syncs via iCloud and connects with WeGlide, SkyLines, Soaring Spot, and other soaring platforms.
